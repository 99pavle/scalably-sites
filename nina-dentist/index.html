About Dr. Nina

With over 15 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Nina founded Bright Smile Dental with a vision to provide exceptional dental care that combines advanced technology with a gentle, patient-focused approach.

After graduating with honors from NYU College of Dentistry, Dr. Nina completed additional training in cosmetic and restorative procedures. She consistently pursues continuing education to bring the latest dental innovations to her patients in New York.

Dr. Nina believes in building lasting relationships with her patients based on trust and open communication. Her caring, compassionate approach makes even the most anxious patients feel at ease.
