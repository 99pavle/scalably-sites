About Dr. Tara

With over 15 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Tara has established herself as one of New York's premier dental professionals. After graduating with honors from NYU College of Dentistry, she founded Bright Smile Dental with a vision to provide personalized, comfortable dental care for all patients.

Dr. Tara continuously advances her knowledge through ongoing education, ensuring her practice offers the most current treatments and technologies available in modern dentistry.

At Bright Smile Dental, we believe in building long-term relationships with our patients based on trust, communication, and exceptional care. Our practice is designed to make your dental experience as comfortable and stress-free as possible.
