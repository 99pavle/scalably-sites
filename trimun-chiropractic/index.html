About Us

Manhattan's Premier Chiropractic Clinic

Founded by Dr. James Trimun, our practice has been a cornerstone of holistic health in Manhattan for over 15 years. At TRIMUN Chiropractic, we believe in addressing the root causes of pain rather than merely treating symptoms.

Our approach integrates traditional chiropractic care with cutting-edge techniques, providing comprehensive solutions for patients from all walks of life—from Wall Street executives to Broadway performers.

What sets us apart is our commitment to personalized care. We take the time to understand each patient's unique history, lifestyle, and goals before developing a tailored treatment plan.
