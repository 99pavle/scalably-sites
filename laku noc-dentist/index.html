About BrightSmile Dental

Modern Dental Practice in New York

Welcome to Your Dental Home

Founded by Dr. Laku Noc, BrightSmile Dental has been providing exceptional dental care to New York residents for over 15 years. Our practice combines state-of-the-art technology with a warm, patient-centered approach.

Dr. Noc graduated with honors from New York University College of Dentistry and has dedicated his career to creating positive dental experiences. His commitment to continuing education ensures that our patients always receive the most advanced and effective treatments available.

Our modern facility in the heart of New York is designed with your comfort in mind. We pride ourselves on creating a welcoming environment where patients of all ages feel at ease.

At BrightSmile Dental, we don't just treat teeth—we care for people. Our team takes the time to listen to your concerns and develop personalized treatment plans that respect your needs and preferences.
