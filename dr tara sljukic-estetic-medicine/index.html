Frequently Asked Questions

What should I expect during my first consultation?

Your first consultation with Dr. Sljukić will be thorough and personalized. We'll discuss your aesthetic goals, medical history, and any concerns you may have. Dr. Tara will examine your facial features and skin condition to develop a customized treatment plan. You'll receive detailed information about recommended procedures, expected results, recovery time, and costs. There's never pressure to proceed—we believe in informed decisions and building trust for a long-term relationship.

How long do the results from aesthetic treatments typically last?

The longevity of results varies depending on the specific treatment, your individual factors (like metabolism, lifestyle, and genetic predisposition), and the treatment area. Generally, dermal fillers can last 6-18 months, while anti-wrinkle injections typically last 3-4 months. Skin treatments may require maintenance sessions for optimal results. During your consultation, Dr. Sljukić will provide a realistic timeline for your specific treatments and recommend the ideal maintenance schedule to preserve your results.

Are aesthetic procedures painful and is there downtime?

Dr. Sljukić prioritizes patient comfort during all procedures. Most injectable treatments involve minimal discomfort, comparable to a quick pinch. We use ultrafine needles, topical numbing creams, and gentle techniques to maximize comfort. Regarding downtime, many treatments allow you to return to normal activities immediately. Some patients may experience mild swelling, redness, or bruising that typically resolves within a few days. More intensive skin treatments might require 1-3 days of social downtime. We'll provide detailed aftercare instructions to ensure optimal healing and results.
