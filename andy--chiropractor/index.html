About Dr. Andrew Harding

Dr. Andrew Harding has been serving the Kansas City community since 1989, bringing over three decades of experience in chiropractic care. His dedication to patient wellness and exceptional results has earned him the prestigious "Chiropractor of the Year" award more than 15 times.

At Chiropractic Healing Center, we believe in addressing the root cause of health issues rather than merely treating symptoms. Our holistic approach combines state-of-the-art technology with time-tested chiropractic techniques to help patients achieve lasting relief and optimal wellness.
