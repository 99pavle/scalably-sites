Who We Are

New York's Premier Dental Practice

VERCELBRE Dental
Caring for New York smiles since 2008

Founded in 2008 by Dr. Michael Vercelbre, our practice has grown to become one of New York's most trusted dental care providers. VERCELBRE Dental combines state-of-the-art technology with personalized, compassionate care.

Our team of highly trained dentists, hygienists, and staff are committed to providing an exceptional dental experience in a comfortable, modern environment. We believe in transparent communication, gentle treatment, and creating lasting relationships with our patients.

Located in the heart of Manhattan, we serve patients from all five boroughs and beyond, providing comprehensive dental care for the entire family. From routine check-ups to complex restorative procedures, we're here for all your dental needs.
