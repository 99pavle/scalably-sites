About BRAVO Dental

Founded by Dr. Michael Bravo, our dental practice has been serving New York City residents for over 15 years. We've built our reputation on providing exceptional dental care in a comfortable, state-of-the-art environment.

At BRAVO Dental, we believe that a healthy smile is crucial to your overall wellbeing. That's why our team of experienced professionals is committed to delivering personalized care using the latest techniques and technology.

Our modern facility in the heart of New York is designed with your comfort in mind, creating a relaxing atmosphere for all your dental visits.
