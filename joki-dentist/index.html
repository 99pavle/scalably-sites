About Dr. Joki

With over 15 years of experience, Dr. Joki has established himself as one of New York's most trusted dental practitioners. After graduating with honors from NYU's College of Dentistry, he continued his education with specialized training in cosmetic and restorative procedures.

At Joki Dental, our mission is simple: to provide each patient with personalized, compassionate care in a comfortable environment. We combine the latest dental technology with a gentle touch to ensure your visit is as pleasant as possible.

Our modern dental practice in the heart of New York City serves patients of all ages, offering comprehensive treatments from routine cleanings to complex restorative work.
