Meet Our Founder

Dr. Milica Lazic

Dr. Milica Lazic is a distinguished dental professional with over 15 years of experience in advanced dental procedures. After graduating with honors from the University of Belgrade's Faculty of Dental Medicine, she pursued specialized training in cosmetic and implant dentistry at prestigious institutions across Europe and the United States.

Her passion for combining artistry with the latest scientific advancements has established Bright Teeth as Belgrade's premier dental clinic. Dr. Lazic is committed to continuous education and regularly attends international conferences to bring cutting-edge techniques to her patients.

With a gentle approach and meticulous attention to detail, Dr. Lazic creates personalized treatment plans that address each patient's unique needs, ensuring exceptional results and lifelong relationships built on trust.
