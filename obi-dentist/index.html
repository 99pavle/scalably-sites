About OBI Dental

Since 2005, OBI Dental has been serving the New York community with exceptional dental care and personalized service. Founded by Dr. Olivia Bennet with a vision to create a dental practice where patients feel comfortable, heard, and well-cared for.

Our team consists of highly trained professionals who stay at the forefront of dental innovations through continuous education. We use the latest technology and techniques to ensure that our patients receive the highest standard of care in a comfortable environment.

At OBI Dental, we believe in building lasting relationships with our patients based on trust, quality care, and exceptional service. We're not just your dental providers; we're your partners in achieving and maintaining optimal oral health.
