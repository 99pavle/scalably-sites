About Dr. Milos

Dr. Milos

Dr. Milos Petrovic founded Milos Dental in New York City with a vision to provide exceptional dental care in a comfortable, state-of-the-art environment.

With over 15 years of experience and advanced training from NYU College of Dentistry, Dr. Milos combines technical expertise with a gentle, patient-centered approach. His commitment to continuing education ensures he stays at the forefront of modern dental techniques and technology.

At Milos Dental, we believe that a healthy smile is essential to overall wellbeing. Our entire team is dedicated to creating a welcoming atmosphere where patients feel valued, informed, and confident in their dental care.
