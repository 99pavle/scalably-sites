Meet Dr. Kristina Evans

A respected figure in New York's dental community, Dr. Kristina Evans combines exceptional clinical expertise with a deeply personal approach to patient care. After graduating with honors from NYU College of Dentistry, she completed advanced training in cosmetic and restorative procedures at the prestigious Pankey Institute.

With over 15 years of experience serving Manhattan's discerning clientele, Dr. Evans has built a reputation for delivering stunning results that enhance both the function and aesthetics of her patients' smiles. Her commitment to continuing education ensures she remains at the forefront of dental innovation.
