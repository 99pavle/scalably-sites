Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dermal fillers typically last?

The longevity of dermal fillers varies depending on the type of filler used, the treatment area, your metabolism, and lifestyle factors. Generally, hyaluronic acid fillers last between 6-18 months. Fillers in areas with more movement (like lips) tend to break down faster than those in less active areas (like cheeks). During your consultation, Dr. Sljukić will discuss the expected duration for your specific treatment plan and recommend follow-up appointments to maintain your results.

Is there downtime after estetic treatments?

Most of our treatments involve minimal downtime, which is why they're often called "lunchtime procedures." With dermal fillers and anti-wrinkle injections, you may experience mild swelling, redness, or bruising at injection sites, but these typically resolve within 24-72 hours. More intensive treatments like deeper chemical peels or certain skin rejuvenation procedures might require 5-7 days of social downtime. Dr. Sljukić will provide you with a detailed aftercare plan and realistic recovery timeline during your consultation.

At what age should I start preventative estetic treatments?

There's no one-size-fits-all answer to this question, as everyone's skin ages differently based on genetics, sun exposure, lifestyle, and skincare habits. Generally, many patients begin considering preventative treatments in their late 20s to early 30s when fine lines start to appear. Starting with good skincare and sun protection is essential at any age. Dr. Sljukić takes an individualized approach, assessing your unique facial anatomy and aging pattern to recommend the right time to begin preventative measures, if appropriate. The goal is always to enhance your natural beauty, not to change your appearance.
