About Dr. Sloba

With over 15 years of experience in advanced dentistry, Dr. Sloba has established a reputation for excellence in New York's dental community. After graduating with honors from NYU College of Dentistry, Dr. Sloba has been dedicated to providing the highest quality dental care using the latest technologies and techniques.

At Sloba Dental, we believe that a healthy smile is essential for overall wellbeing. Our practice combines clinical excellence with a warm, patient-centered approach that puts your comfort first. Our modern facility in the heart of New York City is designed to provide a relaxing environment while delivering exceptional dental care.

Dr. Sloba regularly attends continuing education courses to stay at the forefront of dental innovations, ensuring our patients receive the most advanced and effective treatments available.
