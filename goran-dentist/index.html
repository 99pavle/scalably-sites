About Dr. Goran

Dr. Goran founded Goran Dental with a vision to provide New Yorkers with exceptional dental care in a comfortable, state-of-the-art environment. With over 15 years of experience and continued education in the latest dental techniques, Dr. Goran has established a reputation for excellence in comprehensive dental care.

After graduating with honors from NYU College of Dentistry, Dr. Goran completed advanced training in cosmetic and restorative procedures. His compassionate approach and attention to detail have earned him a loyal patient base throughout New York.

At Goran Dental, we believe that a healthy smile contributes to overall wellness and confidence. Our team is committed to providing personalized care that addresses your unique dental needs while ensuring your comfort throughout every visit.
