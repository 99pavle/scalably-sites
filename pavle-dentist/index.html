About Dr. Pavle

Dr. Pavle founded Pavle Dental with a mission to provide exceptional dental care in a comfortable, patient-focused environment. With over 15 years of experience practicing in New York City, Dr. Pavle has established a reputation for excellence in all aspects of dentistry.

After graduating with honors from New York University College of Dentistry, Dr. Pavle completed advanced training in cosmetic and restorative dentistry. He continuously updates his skills through ongoing education to bring the latest dental innovations to his patients.

Dr. Pavle believes that building relationships with patients based on trust and open communication is the foundation of quality dental care. He takes the time to listen to each patient's concerns and develops personalized treatment plans that address their unique needs and goals.

When not transforming smiles at the practice, Dr. Pavle enjoys exploring New York's diverse culinary scene, attending Broadway shows, and spending time with his family in Central Park.
