About Makii Dental Care

New York's Premier Dental Practice

Founded by Dr. Makii, our practice has been serving the New York community for over 15 years with exceptional dental care that puts patients first.

At Makii Dental Care, we believe that quality dental care should be accessible to everyone. Our modern facility is equipped with cutting-edge technology, allowing us to provide precise diagnostics and comfortable treatments in a relaxing environment.

Dr. Makii and our team of experienced dental professionals are committed to continuing education and staying at the forefront of dental advancements. We take pride in creating lasting relationships with our patients based on trust, compassion, and outstanding results.
