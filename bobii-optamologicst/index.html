About Dr. Bobii Lazic

Leading Ophthalmologist in Belgrade

With over 15 years of experience in ophthalmology, Dr. Bobii Lazic is dedicated to providing exceptional eye care using the most advanced techniques and technologies available.

After graduating with honors from the University of Belgrade Medical School, Dr. Lazic completed his residency at the prestigious Belgrade Eye Clinic before furthering his specialization in refractive surgery at the European Institute of Ophthalmology in Paris.

Dr. Lazic founded Happy Eyes with a mission to combine world-class medical expertise with a compassionate, patient-centered approach that treats each person as an individual with unique needs.

  • Board Certified by the European Board of Ophthalmology

    Recognized for excellence in comprehensive eye care and surgical procedures

  • Fellow of the Royal College of Ophthalmologists

    Demonstrating the highest standards of professional practice

  • Member of the Serbian Medical Association

    Active contributor to advancing ophthalmology practices in Serbia
