About Dr. Plzak

Dr. Daniel Plzak brings over 20 years of specialized experience to his Manhattan dental practice. After graduating with highest honors from NYU College of Dentistry, he completed advanced training in cosmetic and restorative procedures at the prestigious Pankey Institute.

Combining his meticulous attention to detail with a genuine passion for patient comfort, Dr. Plzak has established himself as one of New York's premier dental providers. His philosophy centers on personalized care that addresses both aesthetic desires and functional needs.

Dr. Plzak continually invests in the latest dental technologies and techniques to ensure his patients receive the most advanced, comfortable care available in modern dentistry.
