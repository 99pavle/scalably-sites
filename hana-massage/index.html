Meet Your Therapist

Hana's Healing Journey

With over 12 years of experience in therapeutic massage, I've dedicated my career to helping people find relief, relaxation, and renewal through the power of skilled touch.

After training at the prestigious Alabama Institute of Massage Therapy and completing advanced studies in multiple modalities, I opened Serenity Touch to create a sanctuary where clients can escape the stresses of everyday life and reconnect with their bodies.

My approach combines technical expertise with intuitive understanding to create personalized treatments that address your specific needs. Whether you're seeking relief from chronic pain, athletic recovery, or simply a moment of tranquil escape, my mission is to exceed your expectations with every visit.
