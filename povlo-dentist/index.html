About Povlo Dental Care

Discover why we're one of New York's most trusted dental practices.

New York's Premier Dental Practice

Founded by Dr. Povlo, our practice has been providing exceptional dental care to New York residents for over 15 years. With a passion for patient comfort and advanced dental techniques, Dr. Povlo has built a reputation for delivering outstanding results in a welcoming environment.

Our state-of-the-art facility in the heart of New York City is equipped with the latest technology, allowing us to provide efficient, comfortable, and precise treatments. We believe in preventive care and patient education as the foundations of long-term oral health.

  • Advanced diagnostic and treatment technology
  • Comprehensive care under one roof
  • Comfortable and relaxing environment
  • Flexible scheduling and payment options
  • Emergency appointments available
