About Austin Dental Care

Your trusted dental practice in Austin, TX

Dr. Tom Smith, DDS

For over 15 years, Dr. Tom Smith has been providing exceptional dental care to the Austin community. After graduating with honors from the University of Texas School of Dentistry, Dr. Tom established Austin Dental Care with a mission to combine advanced dental technology with compassionate, patient-centered care.

Our modern practice is designed with your comfort in mind. We use state-of-the-art equipment and the latest techniques to ensure efficient, effective treatments with minimal discomfort.

Dr. Tom and our entire team are committed to ongoing education and staying at the forefront of dental advancements to provide you with the best care possible. We believe in building lasting relationships with our patients based on trust, respect, and open communication.
