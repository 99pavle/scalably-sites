About Dr. John

With over 15 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. John has established Manhattan Dental Care as one of New York's premier dental practices. After graduating with honors from NYU College of Dentistry, he pursued advanced training in cosmetic and restorative procedures.

Dr. John believes in combining traditional dental values with modern techniques to provide exceptional care tailored to each patient's needs. His gentle approach and commitment to continuing education ensure you receive the most advanced and comfortable treatment available.

Manhattan Dental Care was founded in 2008 with a simple mission: to provide New Yorkers with dental care they actually look forward to. Our state-of-the-art facility in central Manhattan is designed for your comfort and convenience.
