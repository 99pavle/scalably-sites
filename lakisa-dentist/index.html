About LAKISA Dental

Founded by Dr. Lakisa Williams, our practice has been serving the New York community for over 15 years. We've built our reputation on combining advanced dental technology with genuine, personalized care.

At LAKISA Dental, we believe everyone deserves a healthy, beautiful smile. Our state-of-the-art facility is designed with your comfort in mind, and our experienced team is committed to providing the highest standard of dental care in a relaxing environment.

We stay at the forefront of dental innovations through continued education and investment in modern equipment, ensuring you receive the most effective and comfortable treatments available.
