About Dr. Obreht

Combining artistry with advanced dental techniques to deliver exceptional results that transform both smiles and lives.

Dr. Obreht established his Brooklyn practice with a vision to redefine dental excellence. With over 15 years of experience and continuous education in the latest dental innovations, he has created a sanctuary where technical precision meets compassionate care.

His philosophy emphasizes personalized treatment plans that address not just immediate concerns but long-term oral health, ensuring that each patient receives care as unique as their smile.
