Meet Dr. Sarah Johnson

With over 15 years of experience in the dental field, Dr. Sarah Johnson has established herself as one of Austin's premier dental professionals, dedicated to providing exceptional care with a gentle touch.

After graduating with honors from the University of Texas School of Dentistry, Dr. Johnson completed advanced training in cosmetic and restorative procedures. Her commitment to continuing education ensures she stays at the forefront of modern dental techniques.

Dr. Johnson founded Smile Bright Care with a vision of creating a dental practice where patients receive personalized care in a comfortable, luxurious environment. Her philosophy centers on building lasting relationships with patients while delivering outstanding results.
