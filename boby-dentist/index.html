About Dr. Boby

Dr. Boby has been serving the New York community for over 15 years with exceptional dental care. After graduating with honors from New York University College of Dentistry, he established NY Bright Smiles with a vision to provide personalized, comprehensive dental care in a comfortable environment.

Our practice combines the latest dental technology with a warm, patient-centered approach. Dr. Boby regularly pursues continuing education to stay at the forefront of modern dentistry, ensuring his patients receive the best possible care.

At NY Bright Smiles, we believe in treating each patient like family. We take the time to listen to your concerns, explain procedures thoroughly, and create treatment plans tailored to your specific needs and goals.
