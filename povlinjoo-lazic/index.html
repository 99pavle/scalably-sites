Dr. Povlinjoo Lazic

With over 15 years of experience in ophthalmology, Dr. Lazic has established himself as one of Belgrade's leading eye care specialists. After completing his medical education at the University of Belgrade, he pursued specialized training in ophthalmology at prestigious institutions across Europe.

Dr. Lazic is passionate about combining traditional eye care practices with innovative technologies to provide the best possible outcomes for his patients. His gentle approach and dedication to excellence have earned him a reputation for exceptional patient care.
