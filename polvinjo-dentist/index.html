About Polvinjo Dental

Founded by Dr. Polvinjo, our dental practice has been serving the New York community for over 15 years with exceptional dental care. Dr. Polvinjo graduated with honors from NYU College of Dentistry and continues to stay at the forefront of dental innovations through ongoing education.

At Polvinjo Dental, we believe in a patient-centered approach where comfort, quality, and personalized care come first. Our modern facility is equipped with the latest technology to ensure efficient and effective treatments for all our patients.

Our team of highly skilled professionals is committed to providing a comfortable and stress-free environment where patients of all ages can receive the dental care they need.
