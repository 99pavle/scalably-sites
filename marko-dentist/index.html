About Dr. Marko & Our Practice

Welcome to Marko's Dental Care, where your oral health and smile are our top priorities. Dr. Marko founded this practice with a simple vision: to deliver exceptional dental care in a comfortable, modern environment where patients feel valued and at ease.

With over 15 years of experience and continuous education in the latest dental techniques, Dr. Marko combines clinical excellence with a gentle, caring approach. After graduating from New York University College of Dentistry, he completed advanced training in cosmetic dentistry and implantology.

Our state-of-the-art facility in the heart of New York features the latest dental technology, allowing us to provide precise, efficient, and comfortable treatment for patients of all ages.
