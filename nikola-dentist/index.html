About Our Practice

Meet Dr. Nikola and our dedicated team

Your Smile Is Our Priority

Welcome to Nikola Dental Care, where we've been serving the New York community for over 15 years. Dr. Nikola founded this practice with a mission to provide exceptional dental care in a comfortable, modern environment.

After graduating with honors from NYU College of Dentistry, Dr. Nikola pursued advanced training in cosmetic and restorative procedures. His commitment to continued education ensures our patients receive the most current, evidence-based treatments available.

Our team believes in building lasting relationships with our patients. We take the time to understand your concerns and goals, creating personalized treatment plans that fit your lifestyle and budget.
