Meet Dr. Mihajlo Lazić

Transforming Smiles in Novi Sad for Over 15 Years

Dr. Mihajlo Lazić is a distinguished dental surgeon renowned throughout Serbia for his expertise in cosmetic and restorative dentistry. After graduating with honors from the University of Belgrade's Faculty of Dental Medicine, he furthered his education with specialized training in Switzerland and the United States.

His philosophy combines technical precision with an artistic eye to create naturally beautiful smiles. Dr. Lazić's compassionate approach puts even the most anxious patients at ease, ensuring a comfortable experience from consultation to completion.

As the founder of Glowing Teeth, Dr. Lazić has created a dental practice that reflects his commitment to excellence, using only the finest materials and latest techniques to deliver outstanding results for every patient.
