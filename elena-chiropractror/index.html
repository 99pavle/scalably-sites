About Dr. Elena Esparza

Elena Esparza, D.C.

Founder & Holistic Chiropractor

With over 15 years of experience in holistic healing, Dr. Elena Esparza brings a unique blend of traditional chiropractic techniques and integrative wellness practices to Just Breathe Healing. Her journey began after experiencing firsthand how chiropractic care transformed her recovery from a sports injury.

Elena's approach centers on the belief that true healing comes from addressing the whole person—not just treating symptoms. She specializes in gentle adjustments that respect your body's wisdom while encouraging its natural healing capabilities.

As a Los Angeles native, Elena is passionate about helping her community reconnect with their bodies and discover sustainable paths to wellness in our busy urban environment.
