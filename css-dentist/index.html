About CSS Dental Care

New York's Trusted Dental Care Provider

Founded by Dr. Catherine S. Smith (CSS), our practice has been serving the New York community for over 15 years with exceptional dental care and personalized service.

At CSS Dental Care, we believe that quality dental healthcare should be accessible to everyone. Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with the latest technology, and our team of experienced professionals is committed to providing comfortable, efficient care in a welcoming environment.

Our approach combines technical excellence with a deep understanding of our patients' needs. We take the time to listen, educate, and develop customized treatment plans that achieve optimal results.
