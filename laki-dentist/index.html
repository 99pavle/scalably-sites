About Laki Dental Care

Dr. Laki and the Laki Dental Team

Committed to Excellence in Dental Care

Founded by Dr. Laki, our practice has been serving the New York community with exceptional dental care for over 15 years. Dr. Laki graduated with honors from NYU College of Dentistry and has continuously enhanced his expertise through advanced training in cosmetic and restorative dentistry.

At Laki Dental Care, we believe in creating a comfortable, stress-free environment where patients of all ages feel welcome. Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with the latest dental technology, allowing us to provide precise diagnoses and effective treatments.

Our team is committed to understanding your unique needs and developing personalized treatment plans that align with your goals and lifestyle. We take pride in building lasting relationships with our patients based on trust, respect, and outstanding results.
