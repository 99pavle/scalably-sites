About Moca Dental Care

Founded by Dr. Moca, our practice has been serving the New York community for over 15 years with exceptional dental care and personalized attention.

Our state-of-the-art facility in the heart of New York City combines cutting-edge technology with a warm, welcoming environment designed to make your dental visit as comfortable as possible.

We believe that everyone deserves access to quality dental care, which is why we offer flexible payment options and work with most major insurance providers.

Dr. Moca and our entire team are committed to continuing education, ensuring we stay at the forefront of dental advancements to provide you with the best care possible.
